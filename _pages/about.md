Hello! I am a PhD candidate at the University of Toronto’s [Faculty of Information](https://ischool.utoronto.ca/), under the supervision of [Rohan Alexander](https://rohanalexander.com/). I study how people work with data!

More specifically, my research uses tools and methods from **information practice scholarship** to study **data work** in **interdisciplinary** settings. I combine these ideas for my dissertation research, where I explore the social practices of data integration in interdisciplinary laboratories. In related, ongoing research collaborations, I investigate data practice in other contexts (like in the public sector and in machine learning research), and study the ways that scholars do interdisciplinary work. My academic work is informed by my professional experience as a data scientist in the public sector.

I developed and teach courses on sampling methods and data visualization, and have led workshops and invited talks on 'Reading and Writing About Data' and 'Critical Data Science'.
